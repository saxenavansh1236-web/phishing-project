Stay safe online

  • Read the domain, not the logo. paypal-login.xyz is not PayPal.
  • Urgent messages ("act in 10 minutes!") are a classic scam trick.
  • Banks and UPI apps never ask for your PIN or OTP, by message or call.
  • Shortened links hide the destination. Scan them before opening.
